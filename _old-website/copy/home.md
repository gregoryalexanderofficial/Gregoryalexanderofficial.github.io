## ABOUT THE ARTIST

"Greg Alexander is a songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, music teacher, and recording artist. He performs on guitar, piano, harmonica, flute and melodica and he writes songs about life, love and the natural world. He also performs with the bands Mystic Revival and Awakened Heart."

"He was fortunate to have grown up in a musical family in Kerville, Texas in the 1970s and 80s. His father, Hilary A. Alexander, was a busker and folk singer who traveled throughout the US and Europe before settling in Kerrville. Greg grew up as part of the folk community behind the Kerrville Folk Festival, and that world has been instrumental in shaping his music and his life."

"Greg has performed at some of Austin's premiere venues including the Continental Club, Antone's and Cactus Cafe, and has played in folk festivals from Colorado to Connecticut. He also recorded an album, "Any Day: Greg Alexander sings the songs of Ryan Fauber," that was released on the Burlington-based label, Jenke Records, and is currently recording tracks with his bands. Greg is also the founder of The Move It Move It, a musical and comedic duo that is popular in the Austin area."
