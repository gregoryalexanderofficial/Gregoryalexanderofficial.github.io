About

Gregory Alexander is a creative facilitator, interdisciplinary artist, and devotee of the improvised moment. A singer and multi-instrumentalist described as a 'deeply gifted vocalist' and 'magician,' he co-creates Austin's quarterly Community Singing Rituals, accompanies dancers and storytellers with live improvised music, and is currently music-directing Sarah Bentley's Impermanence album project. His play spans theater, dance, ceremony, and performance art, with an emphasis on helping people explore confidence, creativity, collaboration, and authentic expression.
